I am a PhD candidate at the LEXplain project (University of Bergen and University of Copenhagen), working on artificial intelligence and legal justification. At the moment, my main research focus is the legal theory of decision-making applied to artificial intelligence (specifically LLMs) making legal decisions.

Alongside the PhD, I lecture on applied AI in law, teach introduction to law, and supervise master's students at the Faculty of Law, UiB.
